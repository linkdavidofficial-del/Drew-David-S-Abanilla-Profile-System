Chapter 9: Clash of the Idols

The hololive EN branch building hummed with its usual afternoon energy. The modern facility, with its sleek design and state-of-the-art streaming equipment, had become a second home to the English-speaking idols of the agency. Sunlight streamed through the large windows, illuminating the common areas where several members lounged between their scheduled activities.

In the spacious lobby, Kronii sat on one of the plush couches, scrolling through her phone with an air of cool indifference. Nearby, IRyS practiced vocal runs, her voice echoing softly through the space as she prepared for an upcoming recording session. Bae bounced past them, full of chaotic energy as always, clutching a energy drink and humming one of her original songs.

"Bae, that's your third one today," Kronii commented without looking up from her phone.

"Fourth, actually!" Bae chirped, taking a defiant sip. "I need the power!"

IRyS laughed, shaking her head. "You're going to be bouncing off the walls during the meeting later."

"That's the plan!"

The building's interior was designed with the idols' needs in mind—multiple streaming rooms, a recording studio, a dance practice area, and most importantly, a gaming lounge that had become the unofficial hangout spot. The walls were decorated with promotional posters of various members, and the air always carried a mix of excitement and creativity.

In their respective streaming rooms, the five members of HoloMyth were wrapping up their individual broadcasts.

Mori Calliope leaned back in her gaming chair, adjusting her headset as she addressed her chat. "Alright, dead beats, that's gonna be it for today's stream. Thanks for hanging out while I worked on these new lyrics. I'll catch you guys in the next one—stay cool, stay you." She threw up a peace sign to her camera before ending the stream with a practiced motion.

Down the hall, Takanashi Kiara was in full idol mode, her energetic voice filling her streaming room. "Okay, KFP employees! Time to clock out for today! Remember, work hard, play hard, and don't forget to stay hydrated! Kikkeriki~!" Her signature sign-off came with an enthusiastic wave before she clicked the end stream button.

In another room, Ninomae Ina'nis stretched her arms above her head, her tentacles swaying gently behind her. "Wah... that was a good art stream. Thanks for keeping me company, everyone. I'll see you all next time. Take care!" Her gentle smile remained as she shut down her broadcast.

Watson Amelia adjusted her detective hat, grinning at her camera. "Alright, gumshoes, mystery solved—for now. Same time next week? Maybe. Probably. We'll see. Ame out!" She gave a casual two-finger salute before ending her stream.

Finally, in the smallest streaming room, Gawr Gura was wrapping up a karaoke session. "Thanks for singing with me, shrimps! That was super fun! Remember to brush your teeth and... um... stay sharky? Yeah! A!" She flashed her signature shark-tooth grin before disconnecting.

One by one, the members of HoloMyth emerged from their respective rooms, each looking slightly worn but satisfied with their work. They naturally gravitated toward the lobby, drawn together by the unspoken camaraderie that came from being the first EN generation.

Calli was the first to collapse onto one of the lobby couches, her long legs sprawled out in front of her. She tilted her head back and stared at the ceiling with a deep sigh. "I'm bored..."

"You're always bored," Kiara immediately shot back, settling onto the couch beside her. She crossed her legs elegantly and pulled out her phone. "You literally just finished a three-hour stream."

"Yeah, and now I'm bored," Calli repeated, turning her head to look at Kiara. "That's how it works."

Ina emerged next, still in her comfy streaming hoodie, and claimed an armchair nearby. "Wah... maybe we should find something to do together?"

"I could go for some snacks," Gura announced, appearing from around the corner. Despite being the shortest member, her presence was always immediately noticeable. She hopped over the back of the couch and landed between Calli and Kiara with surprising agility.

"Gura, there's a gap right there you could have walked through," Ame pointed out, gesturing to the obvious space between furniture pieces.

"But where's the fun in that?" Gura stuck her tongue out playfully.

Kiara laughed, reaching over to ruffle Gura's hair. "Never change, shark."

The five of them settled into comfortable chatter, discussing their streams and sharing funny moments from their chats. The atmosphere was relaxed and familiar—the kind of easy dynamic that came from years of working together and becoming genuine friends.

Meanwhile, on the other side of the building, the members of hololive Advent were making their way through the corridors toward the gaming room. The four of them walked together, their conversation animated and full of the energy that characterized their generation.

Shiori Novella led the pack, her novelist persona evident in the way she dramatically gestured as she spoke. "So I told my chat, 'Listen, you can't expect me to remember every single lore detail I've created,' and they just started spamming the timestamps of when I contradicted myself!"

"That's what you get for making your lore so complicated," Koseki Bijou teased, her petite frame somehow radiating confidence. The purple-haired idol had a mischievous glint in her eye.

Nerissa Ravencroft laughed, her elegant demeanor softening with genuine amusement. "At least your chat pays attention to the lore. Mine just keeps asking me to sing."

"Because you have the voice of an angel, obviously," Shiori replied with a knowing smirk.

"Bau bau!" Fuwawa chimed in enthusiastically, her twin pink hair bouncing as she walked.

"Yeah, bau bau!" Mococo echoed, completing her twin's thought as she always did.

The four of them shared a collective laugh. Despite being the newest generation, Advent had quickly found their rhythm together, their personalities complementing each other in unexpected ways.

"So, how did everyone's streams go?" Nerissa asked as they turned down another corridor.

"Pretty good!" Bijou pumped her fist. "I finally beat that boss I've been stuck on. Chat went wild."

"I finished another chapter," Shiori added. "The angst was peak."

"Bau bau success!" Fuwawa announced.

"We played games with our floofballs!" Mococo clarified.

As they approached the gaming room, Bijou suddenly stopped, causing the others to nearly bump into her. She spun around with a grin spreading across her face. "Hey, I just had an idea!"

"Oh no," Shiori said, though her smile suggested she was intrigued.

"We should play some League of Legends! Custom game!" Bijou's eyes sparkled with competitive fire. "I've been practicing, and I think I could take on anyone in this building."

Nerissa raised an eyebrow. "Bold claim, gem girl."

"I'm serious! It would be fun! We could—" Bijou paused, her eyes widening slightly as she spotted the five members of HoloMyth lounging in the nearby lobby. A truly devious idea formed in her mind. "Wait. What if we challenged Myth?"

Shiori's interest was immediately piqued. "Now that could be entertaining."

"Bau bau challenge!" Fuwawa barked excitedly.

"Myth versus Advent!" Mococo added, pumping her fist.

Nerissa crossed her arms, considering. "Do you think they'd be up for it?"

"Only one way to find out," Bijou said, already marching toward the lobby.

The four Advent members approached the Myth corner of the lobby with varying degrees of confidence. Bijou led the charge, her small stature doing nothing to diminish her presence.

"Hey, Myth!" she called out, catching their attention.

Calli lifted her head from the couch back. "Sup, Advent."

"We're heading to the gaming room to play some League," Bijou announced, cutting straight to the point. "Custom game. And we thought—why not make it interesting? HoloMyth versus hololive Advent. What do you say?"

There was a moment of silence as the five Myth members processed the challenge. Then Gura's eyes lit up like someone had just offered her a lifetime supply of Red Bull.

"Oh, you're ON!" the shark idol jumped up from the couch, pointing dramatically at Bijou. "I've been waiting for a chance to show everyone my skills!"

Ame stood up, adjusting her detective hat with a competitive gleam in her eye. "I'm in. Time to show the new generation how it's done."

"Oh, it's the 'new generation' now?" Shiori challenged, her novelistic flair coming through. "Careful, Watson, your dinosaur is showing."

"Hey!"

Kiara laughed, rising gracefully from her seat. "This could be fun! I'm definitely in. Plus, someone needs to keep Calli alive."

"I don't need protecting," Calli grumbled, but she was already standing up, clearly interested despite her cool demeanor. "But yeah, sure. Could be entertaining."

Ina was the last to rise, her expression serene as always. "Wah... a friendly competition sounds nice. Count me in."

"Bau bau! Let's go!" The twins were already heading toward the gaming room, their excitement infectious.

The ten idols made their way to the gaming lounge—a spacious room filled with high-end gaming PCs, comfortable chairs, and multiple monitors. The room had been designed specifically for moments like this, with enough space for all of them to set up comfortably.

The gaming room was impressive by any standard. Ten identical gaming stations were arranged in two rows facing each other, each equipped with top-of-the-line hardware, mechanical keyboards, and professional-grade gaming mice. RGB lighting cast a soft glow throughout the room, and the air conditioning kept everything at the perfect temperature for long gaming sessions.

The idols claimed their seats, Myth on one side and Advent on the other, with friendly trash talk already beginning.

"Prepare to be demolished," Bijou announced confidently as she booted up her PC.

"Big words for someone I could use as a footrest," Calli shot back with a smirk.

"Height jokes? Really?" Gura jumped to Bijou's defense. "That's my thing to be self-conscious about!"

Everyone laughed as they logged into their accounts and set up the custom game. The atmosphere was competitive but friendly—the kind of rivalry that only existed between friends who genuinely enjoyed each other's company.

"Okay, so how are we doing this?" Ame asked, navigating to the custom game lobby. "Draft or blind pick?"

"Blind pick," Bijou decided. "More chaos that way."

"I like how you think," Shiori approved.

The teams joined the lobby, and the champion select screen appeared. This was where strategy met personality, where each idol's preferences and playstyle would shine through.

On the Myth side, Gura was the first to start talking about champion selection. She leaned forward in her chair, her eyes scanning through the available options with laser focus.

"Okay, okay," she muttered, mostly to herself but loud enough for her teammates to hear. "I'm gonna pick someone water-based! This is my moment!"

Kiara rolled her eyes affectionately. "Of course you are."

"Ooh, what about Nami?" Gura's mouse hovered over the Tidecaller.

"She's a support," Ame pointed out.

"Yeah, oh never mind." Gura's mouse moved on, scanning through the options. "I'm gonna pick Fizz! Gonna go jungle!"

"Let me guess," Calli drawled, "because that trickster's got a trident? Yeah, it fits. And he's short too."

"OY!" Gura spun in her chair to glare at Calli, though the smile tugging at her lips betrayed her amusement. "Why does everyone gotta bring up my height today?"

"Because it's funny," Ame supplied helpfully.

"Traitor," Gura muttered, but she locked in Fizz anyway, clearly pleased with her choice.

Calli scrolled through the tank options, her expression thoughtful. "I'm feeling like being a tank today. Someone's gotta be the frontline."

Before she could lock in her choice, Kiara leaned over with a teasing smile, her fingers already hovering over Caitlyn. "Gonna protect me, Calli?"

There was a beat of silence. Calli's face remained impassive, but there was a slight tint of color on her cheeks. "Whatever. Just don't get caught out."

"That's a yes!" Kiara sang, locking in the Sheriff of Piltover.

Ina had been quietly browsing through the champion roster, her expression peaceful as always. When her turn came, she locked in Aurelion Sol without hesitation.

"Ina..." Ame started, her voice filled with concern.

"No..." Gura added, turning to look at their teammate.

"Oooh..." Kiara drew out the word, somewhere between impressed and worried.

"Why though?" Calli asked bluntly.

"Hush," Ina said with a serene smile, her tone allowing no argument. "I like Sol."

"The space dragon is a bold choice," Ame admitted. "Respect, I guess."

"Wah~" Ina's contentment was palpable.

Finally, Ame studied the remaining options for her role. She needed someone who could get in, cause disruption, and get out. A fighter would do nicely. After a moment's consideration, she locked in her choice with a satisfied nod.

On the Advent side, champion select was equally entertaining.

Shiori cracked her neck from side to side, a smirk playing on her lips as she browsed through the mage roster. "Time to remind everyone why you don't mess with a novelist who knows all the plot twists."

She locked in her mage choice with dramatic flair, clearly ready to control the battlefield with zone control and poke damage.

Nerissa, meanwhile, was hovering over Ahri, the Nine-Tailed Fox. Her choice was perhaps the most predictable one possible.

"Really, Nerissa?" Shiori didn't even look over. "The fox waifu? Could you be any more on brand?"

"She's elegant, she's powerful, and she's beautiful," Nerissa defended, though she was already moving her mouse away. "What's not to love?"

"We have two mages now," Bijou pointed out. "We need balance."

"Ugh, fine." Nerissa scrolled to a different section and locked in Riven, the Exile. "I'll go exp lane then. Hope you appreciate my sacrifice."

"Your sacrifice is noted and appreciated," Shiori said solemnly.

Bijou had been waiting for this moment. Her fingers flew across the keyboard as she locked in Yasuo without a moment's hesitation. "Time to show these senpais what's up."

"Yasuo jungle?" Ame called from across the room. "That's... actually pretty creative."

"I've got a special build," Bijou replied mysteriously. "Just wait."

Finally, the demon dog twins made their selections. Fuwawa carefully chose a tank support, something that could peel and protect while setting up plays. Mococo, naturally, went for a marksman champion that could deal consistent damage from range.

"Bau bau ready!" they announced in unison.

"This team comp is either genius or disaster," Shiori observed. "I'm excited to find out which."

The loading screen appeared, showing both team compositions. In the lobby chat, there were a few final messages:

Then the game loaded, and the Summoner's Rift appeared on all their screens. The familiar terrain, the three lanes, the jungle paths—all of it ready to become a battleground between friends.

"Alright, everyone knows their roles?" Ame asked, taking on the unofficial shotcaller position for Myth.

"Born ready," Gura replied, already moving her Fizz toward the jungle.

"Let's show them what the first generation can do," Kiara added.

On the other side of the room, Bijou was giving similar encouragement. "Remember, early aggression. Don't let them settle into their comfort zones."

"Bau bau aggressive!" Fuwawa barked.

The game timer started counting down: 0:15... 0:10... 0:05...

And then they were off.

The first few minutes of any League match were crucial, and this game was no exception. Both teams spread out across the map, heading to their respective positions.

Gura's Fizz started at the bottom-side jungle, her movements efficient as she cleared the camps. "Okay, smooth clear so far. This is going great!"

In the top lane, Ame and Nerissa faced off, both cautiously farming minions while eyeing each other for any opening. The tension was palpable.

"Come on, Watson," Nerissa called across the room. "Are you just going to farm, or are we going to make this interesting?"

"Oh, you want interesting?" Ame's eyes gleamed behind her glasses.

What happened next could only be described as organized chaos. Ame's champion suddenly lunged forward, abilities flying. Nerissa responded in kind, and within seconds, both champions were locked in an intense duel. Health bars dropped rapidly as both players went all-in, completely committed to the fight.

"AME, IT'S LEVEL 2!" Kiara shrieked from mid lane.

"I KNOW WHAT I'M DOING!" Ame shouted back.

"DO YOU THOUGH?" Calli questioned.

The exchange was brutal and fast. Both champions traded blow for blow until finally, simultaneously, they fell.

First Blood!

An enemy has been slain!

1-1.

"BRO, IT'S JUST EARLY GAME!" Gura's voice echoed through the gaming room, a mix of disbelief and amusement.

"The violence!" Kiara exclaimed, though she was laughing.

Ina, calmly last-hitting minions in the mid lane, let out a soft giggle. "Wah... we're off to an interesting start."

On the Advent side, Shiori was cackling. "That's what I'm talking about! Setting the tone early!"

"Both of you are ridiculous," Bijou muttered, but she was smiling as she cleared her jungle camps.

The aggressive start set the pace for the entire early game. Both teams played with an unusual intensity, each member determined to prove themselves against their generational rivals.

Gura finished clearing her bottom jungle and started moving toward the top side. As she approached, her shark senses tingled—something felt off. She cautiously moved into the river, and that's when she saw her.

Bijou's Yasuo, trying to sneak through the top-side jungle.

"Eh," Gura vocalized her surprise.

"A!" Bijou responded, equally startled.

For a split second, neither moved. Then, as if by mutual agreement, they both lunged at each other.

"BIJOU IN TOP JUNGLE! BIJOU IN TOP JUNGLE!" Gura called out to her team, her fingers flying across her keyboard as Fizz and Yasuo traded abilities.

Ina heard the call and smoothly rotated from mid lane. "That was her biggest mistake," she said with uncharacteristic confidence, her Aurelion Sol gliding toward the skirmish.

Bijou saw Ina incoming and her eyes widened. "AAAAH! HELP! SHIORI, NERISSA, SOMEONE!"

But the backup didn't arrive in time. The 2v1 was swift and decisive.

An enemy has been slain!

"Wah, indeed," Calli commented appreciatively.

Bijou groaned in her seat, respawning back at the fountain. "Okay, that was embarrassing."

"It's fine, it's fine," Shiori reassured her. "It's still early. We can come back from this."

The early game continued with both teams trading advantages. Minions fell, gold accumulated, and experience was gained. Despite the chaotic start, both teams were playing surprisingly well.

As the game progressed into the mid game, the real strategy began to reveal itself. Both teams had champions coming online with their power spikes, and objectives became increasingly important.

"We need to start making plays," Ame announced to her team, pinging the top-lane tower. "That tower is low. Let's pressure it."

"On it," Calli responded, her tank champion lumbering up toward the top lane.

But Advent had similar ideas. Nerissa had successfully pressured the bottom lane, and with Fuwawa and Mococo's coordination, they had whittled down Myth's bottom tower to nearly nothing.

"Push, push, push!" Bijou encouraged from the jungle. "Let's get all the outer towers!"

The result was a fascinating exchange. Within minutes, Advent had claimed the top and bottom outer towers for Myth, while Myth had secured the top and bottom outer towers from Advent.

"It's still even," Kiara observed, studying the map. "This is actually really close."

"Too close," Gura muttered, still farming her jungle. "We need to make a move."

The mid lane became the focal point. Neither team had managed to take the middle outer tower yet, and now both sides recognized its importance. It was time for a team fight.

"Everyone, collapse on mid," Ame ordered. "We're taking this tower."

"Same plan," Shiori told her team. "If they want mid, they're gonna have to fight for it."

Both teams began converging on the middle lane, champions from all corners of the map rotating toward the center. The tension in the gaming room was palpable—this fight would likely determine the momentum for the rest of the game.

"Where's Gura?" Kiara asked, noticing their jungler's absence.

"Bijou, come here!" Nerissa called for her own jungler.

But neither jungler appeared.

Where were they?

Meanwhile, in a completely different part of the map, two small champions were engaged in their own private war.

Gura's Fizz and Bijou's Yasuo had encountered each other near the river—specifically, near a Rift Scuttler that had just spawned. That crab-like creature, usually an afterthought in most games, had become the focus of an intense rivalry.

"It's mine, crystal!" Gura declared, trying to land her abilities on both Bijou and the scuttler.

"Have some tidal waves!"

"Here's some sword!"

"Keep shooting!" Mococo called to her twin.

The fight was incredibly close, with both teams' health bars dropping into dangerous territory. It looked like it could go either way.

And still, in the river, two small champions continued their private duel, completely oblivious to the major team fight happening without them.

The game dragged into the late game, and by now, everyone was fully invested. The friendly trash talk had evolved into focused concentration, with only occasional comments breaking the silence.

"All our towers are gone," Kiara observed, noting that Myth's outer and middle towers had all fallen. "We need to be careful."

"They're going to push for the win soon," Ame agreed, studying the enemy team's movements.

The gold was fairly even, but Advent had a slight advantage in map control. The game was balanced on a knife's edge.

And yet, incredibly, Gura and Bijou were still fighting.

Calli, Kiara, and Ame were doing their best to hold the line against the Fuwawa-Mococo combo, with Shiori providing poke damage and Nerissa looking for an opportunity to engage. The Baron's health slowly decreased as both teams danced around it, nobody willing to fully commit to the fight.

[Myth] Ninomae Ina'nis has slain Baron Nashor!
