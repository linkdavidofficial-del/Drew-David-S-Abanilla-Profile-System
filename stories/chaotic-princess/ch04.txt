Chapter 4: THE KIDNAPPING THAT WASN'T

It was 2 AM when the scream echoed through the palace.

"SHE'S GONE! PRINCESS ELLIANA IS GONE!"

Queen Marianne bolted upright in bed, her heart immediately in her throat. Not again. Please, not again.

But this time was different.

This time, when she burst into the nursery with King Aldric right behind her, she didn't find an empty crib and a chaotic adventure waiting to unfold.

She found Sparky.

Her daughter's beloved Electro Crocodile—the six-foot monster that had somehow become the palace's unofficial guard—was wrapped in thick rope, bound so tightly he could barely move. He thrashed and hissed, electricity sparking uselessly against his restraints.

The window was shattered, glass scattered across the floor like deadly snow.

And Lilia and Rosaria were screaming, their tiny faces red and tear-streaked, pointing at the broken window with trembling hands.

"No," Marianne whispered. "No, no, no, NO—"

"GUARDS!" Aldric's roar shook the palace. "SOUND THE ALARM! THE PRINCESS HAS BEEN TAKEN!"

Within minutes, the entire palace was in chaos—but not the fun kind. Not the Elliana kind.

This was real, terrifying chaos.

Knights poured into the corridors. The Captain of the Guard began barking orders. Mages were summoned to track magical signatures. The Master of Hounds released the tracking dogs.

Prince Lysander appeared in his nightclothes, his face pale. "What happened?"

"Someone took her." Marianne's voice cracked. "Someone took my baby."

Prince Caius was right behind his brother, and for the first time since Elliana had arrived in their lives, he wasn't smiling. His hands were clenched into fists. "We'll find her. We'll get her back."

"She's fifteen months old," Marianne whispered, clutching Aldric's arm. "She's just a baby. What if they hurt her? What if—"

"We'll find her," Aldric repeated firmly, though his jaw was tight. "Mobilize every soldier in the kingdom. Search every house, every road, every—"

"Your Majesties!" A guard burst in. "We found tracks leading from the window! Two sets of adult footprints, heading toward the East Road!"

"FOLLOW THEM!"

The palace erupted into organized pandemonium. Soldiers mounted horses. Mages prepared tracking spells. The royal family gathered in the war room, where maps were already being spread across the table.

Marianne paced, her hands shaking. "She must be so scared. She's never been away from us like this, what if she's crying, what if—"

Lysander placed a gentle hand on his mother's shoulder. "Mother. This is Elliana we're talking about."

"I know, but—"

"Remember when she convinced the head chef to let her 'help' with dinner and nearly burned down the kitchen?"

"That's not—"

"Remember when she taught Sparky to play fetch with the palace guards' helmets?"

Despite everything, Marianne let out a wet laugh. "She's still just a baby."

"She's Elliana," Caius said firmly. "She's going to be fine. Actually..." He paused. "I'm more worried about whoever took her."

"CAIUS!"

"I'm serious! Do you remember what she did to Duke Remington when he tried to lecture her about 'proper decorum'?"

"She put honey in his ceremonial wig."

"Exactly! And these are kidnappers. People who thought they could just... take her." Caius shook his head. "They have no idea what they've gotten themselves into."

Two hours later.

The main gates of the palace opened.

At first, the guards on duty thought they were seeing things.

Then they rubbed their eyes.

Then they called for backup, because what they were seeing was impossible.

Walking up the main road—no, stumbling up the main road—were two figures.

Two grown men, both wearing dark cloaks and masks, both looking like they'd aged ten years in the span of two hours.

They were carrying something.

No—someone.

Princess Elliana sat nestled on a pile of cushions that the kidnappers had apparently been forced to arrange for her comfort. She looked absolutely pristine. Not a hair out of place. Her little dress was still perfectly clean.

And she was smiling.

Not just smiling. Grinning. That specific grin that every palace servant had learned to fear.

As they approached the gates, her voice rang out, clear and sing-song:

"SIX-SEVEN! SIX-SEVEN! SIX-SEVEN!"

The kidnappers flinched at each repetition like they were being physically struck.

"PRINCESS ELLIANA HAS RETURNED!" the gate guard shouted.

Within moments, the entire royal family came running.

"ELLIANA!" Marianne practically flew down the palace steps, her nightgown billowing behind her.

The kidnappers—because what else could they be—immediately dropped to their knees, still holding the cushions with Elliana perched on top like a tiny, smug queen.

"Please," one of them croaked, his voice hoarse. "PLEASE. Take her back."

"We're begging you," the other one added, and he looked like he was on the verge of tears. "We can't—we just—PLEASE."

"SIX-SEVEN! SIX-SEVEN!" Elliana continued cheerfully.

Both kidnappers shuddered.

Aldric stepped forward, his hand on his sword. "You DARE kidnap the princess and then—"

"WE'RE SORRY!" the first kidnapper wailed. "We didn't know! We thought—we thought she was just a baby!"

"SHE'S A DEMON!" the second one shouted. "A SMALL, TERRIFYING DEMON!"

"SIX-SEVEN! SIX-SEVEN! SIX-SEVEN!"

"MAKE IT STOP!" The first kidnapper was definitely crying now. "We'll confess to everything! We'll tell you who hired us! We'll accept any punishment! JUST PLEASE TAKE HER BACK!"

Marianne blinked. Then blinked again. Then looked at her daughter, who was still grinning that absolutely unhinged grin.

"Elliana, sweetheart... what did you do?"

Elliana's grin somehow widened. "Six-seven! Six-seven! Six-seven!"

"What does that even MEAN?!" one of the kidnappers screamed.

"IT DOESN'T MEAN ANYTHING!" the other one sobbed. "That's what makes it WORSE! We asked her! We BEGGED her to tell us what it means! She just kept SAYING IT!"

"For TWO HOURS!" the first one added. "TWO. HOURS. The same two words! OVER AND OVER AND OVER!"

Lysander covered his mouth, and his shoulders started shaking.

He was laughing.

Caius wasn't even trying to hide it. He was doubled over, wheezing.

"Six-seven," Elliana said one more time, very deliberately, and both kidnappers whimpered.

"She wouldn't stop," one of them whispered, staring into the middle distance like a soldier recounting war trauma. "We tried everything. We tried ignoring her. She got louder. We tried feeding her. She threw the food and said 'six-seven.' We tried threatening her—"

"YOU THREATENED MY DAUGHTER?!" Marianne's voice could have shattered glass.

"We didn't mean it! We just—she's a BABY, we thought—but she just LOOKED at us and said 'six-seven' again and we—we just—"

"Broke," the other one finished. "We broke. Completely. We're ruined men."

The palace guards were trying very hard to maintain professional composure.

They were failing.

Several were shaking with suppressed laughter. One had to turn away entirely.

Aldric cleared his throat, still gripping his sword. "Who hired you?"

"Baron Wickfield," the first kidnapper said immediately. "He wanted leverage against the crown. Thought kidnapping the 'simple' princess would be easy." He laughed, a slightly unhinged sound. "Easy. EASY. There's nothing easy about that child."

"SIX-SEVEN!" Elliana contributed helpfully.

Both kidnappers flinched so hard they nearly fell over.

"Take them to the dungeons," Aldric ordered the guards. "We'll deal with Baron Wickfield in the morning."

The kidnappers went willingly—eagerly—practically running toward the dungeons like they were the safest place in the kingdom.

As they were led away, one of them looked back at Elliana one last time.

"You're a monster," he whispered, awe and terror in his voice. "A beautiful, terrifying monster."

Elliana waved bye-bye.

Once they were gone, Marianne swept Elliana into her arms, cushions and all, hugging her tight. "Don't you EVER scare me like that again!"

"But I won," Elliana said, genuinely confused. "I psychologically defeated my captors and returned safely. Isn't that a good thing?"

"That's not—you can't just—" Marianne stopped, because what could she even say? Her daughter had somehow turned a kidnapping into a victory.

"What does 'six-seven' mean?" Lysander asked, still grinning.

Elliana's smile turned mysterious. "You'll never know~"

Caius high-fived her tiny hand. "You're my hero."

"I know."

Aldric looked at his wife. His wife looked at him.

They both looked at their daughter, who was currently being swarmed by her sisters. Lilia and Rosaria had toddled over, crying happy tears, trying to hug Elliana while she patted their heads like a benevolent dictator.

"She's going to be the death of me," Marianne whispered.

"She's going to be the death of all of us," Aldric agreed.

"SIX-SEVEN!" Elliana shouted one last time, just because she could.

Somewhere in the dungeons, two grown men whimpered.

The palace staff added a new rule to the survival guide:

Rule #6: Never, EVER kidnap Princess Elliana. It's not worth it.
