Chapter 2: THE CROCODILE INCIDENT

Three weeks had passed since the "Big Booba Incident"—as it was now referred to in hushed whispers among the palace staff.

Queen Marianne sat in her private study, quill in hand, adding yet another entry to what had become less of a baby journal and more of a chronicle of unprecedented chaos.

Week One: Elliana spoke her first word at birth. Still unclear if this is divine blessing or curse.

Week Two: Elliana has begun making eye contact with servants and appears to be... judging them? One maid swears she heard her mutter "inefficient" when observing the laundry process.

Week Three: Elliana has learned to—

A frantic knock interrupted her writing.

"Your Majesty!" A maid burst through the door, her face pale and hair disheveled. "It's the princess—Princess Elliana—she—we can't—you need to come NOW!"

Marianne's heart stopped. She dropped her quill and gathered her skirts, running through the palace corridors with the maid. "What happened? Is she hurt?"

"No, Your Majesty, she's—we don't know how to explain—"

They reached the nursery.

The scene was pandemonium.

Princess Lilia and Princess Rosaria lay in their ornate cribs, both wailing at the top of their lungs. Wet nurses fluttered about uselessly. The head nursemaid stood in the center of the room, spinning in circles, her face a mask of pure panic.

And Elliana's crib was empty.

"WHERE IS SHE?!" Marianne shrieked, rushing to the empty crib as if her daughter might somehow be hiding under the blankets.

"We don't know, Your Majesty! One moment she was sleeping, and the next—"

"GONE?! A THREE-WEEK-OLD INFANT IS GONE?!"

King Aldric chose that exact moment to appear in the doorway, likely drawn by his wife's screaming. "What's all this commotion about—"

"OUR DAUGHTER IS MISSING!"

The King's eyes widened. "Which—"

"ELLIANA!"

"Of course it's Elliana," Aldric muttered, already moving into crisis mode. "Sound the alarm. Search the palace. She's three weeks old, she can't have gone far—"

"INCOMING! INCOMIIIIING!"

A butler's voice echoed from somewhere down the hall, high-pitched and absolutely terrified.

Then came the sounds.

Thump. Thump. Thump.

Heavy footfalls. Something large. Something scaly.

And over it all, a sound that made Marianne's blood run cold:

High-pitched, gleeful, baby laughter.

"Ehehehehehe! FASTER! FASTER, you overgrown handbag!"

The King and Queen sprinted into the hallway.

What they saw would haunt them for the rest of their lives.

Charging down the marble corridor—the one with the priceless Avernian tapestries and the imported Celestian vases—was an Electro Crocodile.

Six feet of scaled, crackling death. Blue-white electricity arced across its body, leaving scorch marks on the walls. Its jaws could snap a grown man in half. Adventurers' Guild postings rated them as C-Rank threats requiring a minimum four-person party to take down safely.

And riding on its back, tiny fists gripping the ridges of its scales, was Princess Elliana.

She was cackling.

"YEEEEEHAW! MOVE IT, SPARKY!"

The crocodile bellowed—a sound like thunder compressed into a roar—and somehow accelerated.

"ELLIANA!" Marianne screamed.

Down the hall, Prince Caius stood pressed against a doorframe, laughing so hard he'd collapsed to his knees. "THIS IS THE BEST DAY OF MY LIFE!"

Next to him, twelve-year-old Prince Lysander had his face buried in both hands. "Why," he muttered. "Why us. What did our family do to deserve this."

"GUARDS!" King Aldric bellowed. "STOP THAT CREATURE! SAVE THE PRINCESS!"

Knights poured into the corridor from both ends, weapons drawn. The Electro Crocodile skidded around a corner—how was it drifting?!—taking out a decorative suit of armor in the process.

Elliana continued cackling, looking for all the world like she was having the time of her short life.

"FORM UP!" Captain Helena, head of the Royal Guard, organized her knights into a barrier formation. "Protect the princess!"

The crocodile charged straight at them.

"SHIELDS!"

At the last second, the beast leaped—a six-foot vertical jump that should have been physically impossible—sailing clear over the knights' heads.

Elliana whooped. "TEN OUT OF TEN! STICK THE LANDING!"

The crocodile crashed down, electricity sparking across the floor, and continued its rampage.

"She's going to die!" Marianne clutched her husband's arm. "Aldric, our daughter is going to be electrocuted and eaten!"

"She seems... fine?" Aldric said weakly, watching as Elliana somehow shifted her weight, and the crocodile—obeyed—turning down a different corridor.

"IS SHE STEERING IT?!"

More knights appeared. Someone shouted about getting nets. Someone else suggested a lightning mage. Pure chaos.

The crocodile—Elliana had apparently named it Sparky in the span of thirty seconds—thundered through the East Wing, the Portrait Gallery, and somehow ended up in the Grand Ballroom.

It was there that Captain Helena finally got ahead of them, sword drawn, positioning herself between the creature and the exit.

"HALT, BEAST!"

The Electro Crocodile slowed, electricity crackling dangerously.

Elliana, still perched on its back, looked directly at the Captain.

Then, with the most innocent baby expression imaginable, she raised one tiny hand.

And patted the crocodile's head.

"Good boy, Sparky. Good boy."

The creature—the C-Rank threat, the river demon that had terrorized fishing villages—made a sound that could only be described as a purr.

The entire ballroom went silent.

"Did..." Captain Helena lowered her sword slightly. "Did that crocodile just... purr?"

Elliana grinned—a toothless baby grin that somehow managed to look sinister. "We're bonded now. He's mine."

"YOU CAN'T BOND WITH A CROCODILE!" Marianne shrieked from the doorway.

"Watch me!"

"YOU'RE THREE WEEKS OLD!"

"Details!"

King Aldric pinched the bridge of his nose. "Someone please explain to me how an infant acquired a wild Electro Crocodile."

None of the servants had answers.

Caius was still laughing hysterically in the background. "Can I ride it next? PLEASE can I ride it next?!"

"NO!" Marianne and Aldric shouted in unison.

Lysander approached cautiously, studying his baby sister with a mix of fascination and horror. "Elliana... how did you even get here? You can't walk yet."

Elliana's grin widened. "Rolled."

"You rolled out of your crib, through the palace, all the way to—where did you even find this thing?!"

"The moat."

"WE HAVE AN ELECTRO CROCODILE IN OUR MOAT?!" Aldric turned to his guards. "WHY DO WE HAVE AN ELECTRO CROCODILE IN OUR MOAT?!"

Various guards exchanged uncomfortable looks.

"So," Elliana said cheerfully, still perched atop Sparky like a tiny conqueror. "Can we keep him?"

"ABSOLUTELY NOT!"

"He followed me home. He's basically already ours."

"THAT IS NOT HOW THIS WORKS!"

Sparky the Electro Crocodile chose that moment to open his jaws and release a small burst of electricity—not threatening, almost... playful?

The nearby chandelier flickered.

Marianne looked at her husband. Her husband looked at the crocodile. The crocodile looked at Elliana. Elliana looked at everyone with those big, innocent baby eyes.

"Please?" she said, in the sweetest voice imaginable.

"I need wine," Marianne whispered. "I need so much wine."

"It's not even noon," Aldric replied.

"I DON'T CARE."

From the corridor, Caius's voice echoed: "THIS IS THE BEST SISTER EVER!"

Lysander just sighed, already mentally composing his future memoirs: Growing Up With A Chaos Gremlin: A Prince's Survival Guide.
