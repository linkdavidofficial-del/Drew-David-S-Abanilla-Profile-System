Chapter 3: Welcome to Your New Life

Soldiers armed with state-of-the-art energy rifles stood like statues along the reinforced corridors, their faces hidden behind polished ballistic visors. Overhead, high-tech gun-drones hummed with a predatory whir, their red laser optics tracking every micro-movement of the inmates below. The sheer weight of the power-dampening chains made every step a grueling chore, dragging heavily against the cold concrete. Guards routinely slammed uncooperative bodies against the steel walls while automated turrets pivoted on oiled gears. Red Ash was forced to wait her turn as they took the prisoners in, one by one, for processing. She stood relaxed, unfazed by the oppressive atmosphere, watching the systematic subjugation of her fellow mutants with an amused detachment.

When her number was finally called, she was marched into a stark, windowless hall. A severe prison officer sat behind a heavy metal desk, looking up from a screen to examine her with cold, calculating eyes.

"State your name, age, sex, and power," he commanded, his voice devoid of any human warmth.

Red Ash sat down in the bolted-to-the-floor metal chair, the heavy chains rattling against her frame. She looked directly at him, a slow, infuriating smile pulling at the corners of her lips.

"Red Ash, age 43, female, possesses temporary physical strength enhancement," she declared with a sharp smirk.

The woman was a striking, dangerous presence in the room. Her vibrant crimson hair framed a face marked by sharp black horns that caught the harsh fluorescent light, and her slit-like pupils remained locked on the officer. She wore her tattered red jacket open, projecting a supreme, knowing confidence, as if she harbored deep, ulterior motives for allowing herself to be captured. The officer raised an eyebrow, adjusting his digital tablet as he recorded her vitals. The system catalogued her intake file with clinical detachment, noting her dense muscle mass, the physiological anomalies of her horns, and an unpredictable psychological profile.

The officer standing beside the desk scoffed loudly, eyeing her file with blatant disrespect. "Forty-three. Most of your kind don't live past twenty."

Red Ash chuckled softly, the sound low and raspy. "Depends on whether you've read my file. Which I doubt," she stated, raising her chained hands in a mock gesture of surrender, her expression entirely unfazed. "Just a lone wolf type of criminal. Got caught, simple as that."

She casually slung one long leg over the other, leaning back into the hard chair with an arrogant smirk. The processing officer scoffed, his gaze lingering on her horns with deep-seated distaste.

"Lone wolf, huh? We'll see how long that lasts in OMP-Site," he said, tapping the tablet with aggressive finality. "The gangs in here eat up 'lone wolves' for breakfast." He scribbled a final note before motioning sharply to the heavily armed guards waiting by the door. "Cuff her with additional reinforced restraints—strength enhancers don’t get standard issue."

As a pair of heavy-duty, dense-alloy cuffs clamped tightly onto her thick wrists, the officer leaned forward over his desk, his eyes narrowing. "Just a heads-up—the Psycho-Cubes have had vacancies lately. Step out of line even a fraction, and we'll reserve you a cozy, isolated cell right there."

"Nah, I ain't a hassle," Red Ash replied, waving her bound hands in a dismissive gesture. "I don't have any particular needs. Just something I can lay down on, and a private room is perfectly fine." She chuckled again, her burning red eyes and black, slit-pupiled gaze settling heavily on the officer's face. "So you don't have to worry about me stepping out of line~." She slowly licked her lips, her expression purely predatory.

The officer’s face hardened into stone at her smug, mocking demeanor. He leaned back in his chair, folding his arms tightly across his chest. "We don’t worry about muties like you. We deal with them." He signaled to a nearby guard whose arms and chest were heavily modified with cybernetic augmentations. "Strip-search, standard scan for concealed implants or contraband—then throw her in Cell Block B-7. Let's see how long that carefree act lasts once she meets her new cellmate."

As the massive cybernetic guard stepped forward, his hydraulic joints hissing as he reached for her arm, the officer added with icy finality: "And if you lick your lips at my staff again? You’ll wake up with no tongue left in your head."

"Hm, feisty," she cackled, standing up in a single, smooth, casual motion that defied the weight of her dampening restraints. She raised both her hands willingly and closed her eyes with a relaxed hum.

The cybernetic guard didn't appreciate the attitude; he yanked her forward roughly, shoving her body toward the dark processing hallway. The officer's voice echoed down the corridor after her. "Welcome to your new life, mutie. Pray it’s a short one."

The heavy hydraulic blast doors sealed shut behind her with a deafening boom, locking her deeper into the OMP-Site’s labyrinthine nightmare.
