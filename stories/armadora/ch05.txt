Chapter 5: The Sky Falls

The pods descended like sleek teardrops, cutting a violent path through the unnaturally thick, heavy layer of smog and storm clouds. There were millions of them. And as the seconds ticked away, millions more continued to breach the upper atmosphere, an endless, terrifying cascade of dark metal that actively blotted out the sun and plunged the landscape into sudden twilight.

Up in the central command tower, the facility's high-level tech operatives were working at a frantic, desperate pace, their fingers flying across glowing keyboards as their consoles began to glitch from the kinetic interference. One of the operators glanced up from his screen, his eyes tracking the external feed before shifting directly to the overhead sky.

Instantly, the terminal red warning lights began to blare, bathing the control room in a pulsing, bloody glow.

"What the hell is going on?" a senior technician barked, his eyes blinking in sheer confusion as the systems began to crash around him. "Look out the window! Look at the sky!"

A female officer bolted upright from her desk, her boots clicking sharply against the metal flooring as she rushed toward the thick, bulletproof glass observation pane. Her eyes widened into circles of dread. "What... what is that..."

Behind her, the rest of the crew scrambled to their monitors, trying to force the long-range sensors to register the anomaly. One of them began shouting into his headset, his voice cracking with panic. "We have a critical situation! There are large-scale projectiles—multiple of them! They're hitting terminal velocity and heading straight for the site!"

"Call the warden! Now!"

"I'm detecting seven inbound coming over the eastern sector—no, wait, eight! Nine! Twelve! God, they just keep multiplying! More are coming! They're dropping from orbit!"

The primary comms channel hissed to life, and the deep, authoritative roar of the Warden exploded through the operators' headsets. "What the hell is the situation up there? Report!"

"Several unknown vessels are heading directly toward our coordinates, sir!" the operator yelled back, his hands shaking as he tried to stabilize the tracking telemetry. "It’s... it’s not a prison break, Warden! It’s a full-scale invasion!"

"What?" The Warden’s voice dropped into a brief, horrified vacuum of silence before he recovered. "Lock the place down! Lock it down right now!"

The entire multi-billion-dollar facility went into instant, catastrophic lockdown, foot-thick hydraulic blast doors slammed down throughout the corridors like metal guillotines, sealing off every major sector with bone-rattling force. The standard white fluorescent tubes died instantly, replaced by the violent, rotating flash of red emergency strobes. The air inside the cell blocks grew heavy and thick with the smell of scorched electronics as the central intercom signals began to scramble and distort.

A deafening klaxon began to wail through the ventilation shafts, a maddening screech that signaled terminal danger. Then, a deep, synthesized automated voice filled the halls, echoing into every concrete chamber.

"Prisoners: All power cells are to report to designated lockdown areas immediately. OMP-Site is under a terminal security emergency. Repeat: OMP-Site is under a security emergency."

"Oh... that can't be good..." a soft, trembling whisper drifted through the vents from one of the neighboring cells, the previous bravado of the inmate instantly shattered.

The entire facility hummed with an ominous, vibrating tension, every single eye—human and mutant alike—focused instinctively toward the ceiling, waiting for the sky to fall.

Inside cell B-7, Red Ash smirked beneath the heavy frame of her tactical gas mask. A low, guttural cackle bubbled up from her throat, rattling the internal filters. "Here they come, baby," she whispered, her voice laced with venomous delight. "Here they come!"

A split second later, a low, incredibly violent rumble shook the bedrock of the prison. The orbital bombardment had begun. The supersonic shells tore through the atmosphere with the sound of a thousand tearing canvases, rocking the entire facility down to its foundations. OMP-Site was built out of reinforced titan-alloy to withstand standard military-grade artillery, but this endless, pounding from orbit was an entirely different beast. The concussive shockwaves rattled the cells, causing dust, concrete chips, and old paint to rain down from the ceiling.

Outside the block, the chaos among the security forces was absolute. Fully armored tactical soldiers, their bodies encased in thick metal plating, scurried across the corridors like ants in a disturbed nest, their boots slamming against the floor as they shouted conflicting orders over the blaring klaxons.

"Get the automated drones activated! Circle the perimeter!"

"Move, move, move!" another squad leader roared, charging down the central spine of the block with ten heavily armed guards trailing tightly behind him. "Everyone get outside and see what the hell is going on out there!"

"Where is the automated defense system? Why aren't the exterior batteries firing?!"

"Activate the heavy turrets! Manual override now!"

Down in the dark rows of cells, the sudden violence of the bombardment drove the captive mutants into a state of blind, hysterical panic. Without their powers, they were helpless against the vibrating walls. Men and women began clawing frantically at the reinforced steel doors until their fingers bled, screaming in unbridled rage or terror. Others curled tightly into fetal positions on their metal bunks, pressing their faces into their arms to muffle their own weeping.

But Red Ash was doing the exact opposite.

She cackled wildly under her mask, unaffected by the thick neuro-suppressant gas that was now slowly dissipating, thinned out by the sudden pressure changes running through the ventilation system. She rose from her metal bed with a slow, powerful stride, her long legs carrying her straight toward the thick front door of her cell.

With a brutal, heavy-handed stroke, she slammed her chained fists against the thick metal barrier, her laughter echoing like a maniac through the small viewing slit. Her smile beneath the mask was undoubtedly, unhinged.

"Welcome to the new age, fuckers!" she shouted, her voice cutting through the alarms.

She arched her muscular back outward, lolling her head back as the sheer euphoria of the violence consumed her. Her lungs strained violently against the seals of the gas mask as she let out a booming, uninhibited laugh. She began to twirl in place, gripping the heavy horizontal locking bar of the door like a pole, spinning her frame around until she nearly collapsed against the concrete from sheer, breathless hysteria.

She snapped her spine upright again, her glowing red eyes locking onto the empty hallway outside her cell.

"Your precious superiority of human engineering won't stand against what's coming..." she hissed, her voice dripping with malice as she chuckled darkly. "You see... My Boss wants some specific assets. He wants some choice muties to be recruited into the fold~ and I heard there are a lot of..."

A brief, heavy silence hung in her voice as she paused, her shoulders shaking with amusement.

"... Fresh Meat in this shithole."

Her theatrical tirade was abruptly cut short as the small metallic speaker box mounted on the ceiling overhead buzzed to life with a sharp spike of feedback. The distorted, furious voice of the Warden himself cut through the noise. "Inmate Red Ash. Your actions and statements are in direct violation of terminal prison protocols. This treasonous behavior will be addressed with maximum prejudice as suc—"

Before the Warden could finish his sentence, Red Ash swung her fist in a blunt, lightning-fast arc. Her knuckles collided with the overhead speaker housing with bone-shattering force, crushing the metal casing and tearing the wiring out of the wall in a shower of sparks.

"Fuck you!" she spat, wiping a stray wire from her shoulder.

Suddenly, the heavy hydraulic mechanism of her cell door groaned. The electronic lock flashed green as the command tower attempted to deploy a neutralization squad. The blast door slid open with a heavy hiss, revealing a corridor bathed in red light. Three heavily armed guards stepped directly into the threshold, their pulse-rifles leveled squarely at her chest. Their cold, black tactical uniforms and mirrored visors hid their faces, revealing nothing but their sharp, grim eyes locked onto her target profile.

"Put your hands on the wall and face away from the door," the lead guard commanded, his pulse-rifle humming as the energy cell charged. "You have five seconds to comply before we use lethal force."

Red Ash stared down the barrels of the weapons, her eyes flashing with a terrifying, maniacal glee behind her visor.

"I think you boys don't have the authority to order me around anymore~"
