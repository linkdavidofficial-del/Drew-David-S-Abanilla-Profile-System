Chapter 4: Showtime in the Vapors

A short while later, after the clinical, invasive protocols of the strip-search and automated scanning arrays were completed, Red Ash was marched down the corridor of Cell Block B-7. The heavy reinforced door of her assigned unit slid open.

"Sweet, I dig it," Red Ash smirked, stepping inside and looking around the bleak, grey enclosure before sitting down on the low metal bunk. She chuckled softly to herself. "Looks like a bed—and a big, spacious room," she murmured with thick, dripping sarcasm. She turned her head toward the exit. "And a door. How incredibly thoughtful of them."

She sat back against the wall, humming a quiet tune as she crossed her legs and swayed onto the mattress with pure nonchalance. The reinforced cell door slammed shut with a violent, metallic clang, its heavy locking pins hissing as they pneumatically sealed her inside the concrete tomb. Almost immediately, the walls began to hum with a low, vibrating frequency—the energy of the facility's localized suppression fields, designed to completely deaden mutant abilities.

A flat, synthesized robotic voice crackled over the small ceiling intercom: "Inmate behavior will be monitored at all times. Any infractions will result in immediate disciplinary action."

The lone fluorescent light tube above her flickered erratically, casting long, harsh shadows across the cramped, damp space. The cell offered nothing but the two cold metal slabs serving as bunks and a rusted steel toilet bolted directly to the floor.

From the adjacent cell, a low, rasping chuckle began to echo through the ventilation shafts. "Ohhhh, fresh meat~ Better pray they don’t toss you into my block next, Red."

Red Ash merely chuckled, leaning her upper back against the cold concrete wall. Her smile stretched from ear to ear, completely relaxed as she idly examined her fingernails. With a slow tilt of her head, she scoffed toward the vent, "Pray? Hah! I don't pray, you dog."

Her gaze drifted upward to the stained ceiling as she continued to hum her tuneless melody. She began tapping her finger against the flat surface of the metal bed—a single sharp tap, then a quick double tap, another single, and then a deliberate triple tap.

"Well... this block won't exist much longer anyway, or even the entire facility to even survive," she mused aloud, her eyes glancing mockingly at the overhead intercom speaker. Slowly, she lay back, staring up into the flickering light. "It'll be a complete blast."

The intercom suddenly crackled violently with harsh static, and the deep, oppressive voice of The Enforcer boomed through the speaker—a sound like grinding gravel and heavy gunfire. "Inmate Red Ash. Final warning: Disciplinary action inbound."

A sharp, sinister hiss filled the small room as a dense, pale neuro-suppressant gas began flooding into the cell from concealed vents near the floor, specifically formulated to paralyze mutant neural pathways and force compliance. Simultaneously, the walls vibrated with a sickening intensity as the localized psi-dampeners cranked to maximum power, making the air itself feel heavy as lead. Whoever was monitoring her had clearly lost patience.

She just chuckled, the sound muffled by the rising vapors.

From the neighboring cells down the block, other inmates began frantically banging their fists against the steel doors, howling in a mix of manic agitation, terror, and mocking laughter. The same teasing voice from the vent cut through the rising din: "Oho~? Looks like someone’s got plans. Tell me more... before they melt your brain out~"

Red Ash cackled, her eyes widening in the dim light. "Why should I?" She grinned wildly. "Listen, you arrogant bastards. I'm not here alone trapped. I'm here to set this place on fire... literally."

Letting out a full, unbridled laugh, she raised her chained hand to her mouth. With a slick movement of her tongue, her fingers withdrew a tiny, circular object no larger than an ant that had remained undetected by the intake scanners. Pinching the micro-device between her fingertips, it emitted a sharp, high-pitched mechanical whir. The tiny object rapidly unfolded, expanding outward in a blur of shifting metallic plates and seals until it formed a full, heavy-duty tactical gas mask.

She sat up straight, then slouched casually back against the frame of the bed, securing the straps of the mask tightly over her face. Even behind the reinforced ocular frames of the visor, her smirk was visible. Her eyes flared, glowing a hot, dangerous crimson through the darkness.

"Showtime..."

She lowered her hand back onto the cold metal of the bed and tapped the surface exactly twice.

At first, there was silence. The neuro-suppressant gases continued their slow, heavy seep into the cell, pooling around her boots, though her mask left her grinning, perfectly unharmed and breathing clean oxygen.

Then, a sound tore through the lower foundations of the facility—a hulking, deep BOOM—strange, resonant, and unexpected. The floorboards vibrated. Then another explosion rocked the structure. Two more followed in rapid succession, sounding like supersonic cracks erupting from the high atmosphere, as if large, solid objects had been dropped from orbital range.

Seven more thunderous booms shattered the air. Then twenty. Then thirty. The sounds of heavy, kinetic projectiles tore past the upper atmosphere at impossible, screaming speeds, hurtling straight down toward the heart of the OMP-Site.
