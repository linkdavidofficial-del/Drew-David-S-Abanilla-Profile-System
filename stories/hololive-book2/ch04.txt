Part Four: Bermuda Triangle Nightmares (Council + IRyS)

The first thing Sana noticed upon regaining consciousness was that she was wet.

Very wet.

And floating.

And also approximately fifty times her normal size.

"Oh good, I'm awake," she muttered, her voice echoing slightly across the water. "And apparently I auto-expanded to survival mode. Thanks, cosmic powers."

She took stock of her situation. She was floating in the middle of the ocean—no land visible in any direction—and currently serving as a makeshift life raft for the unconscious members of Council and IRyS, who were sprawled across her like she was the world's most uncomfortable pool float.

Their luggage, miraculously, had ended up scattered across her enlarged form as well, along with debris from their section of the plane.

"Right," Sana said to herself. "This is fine. This is totally fine. Just floating in the middle of nowhere with five unconscious people on top of me. Normal Monday."

Kronii was the next to wake, jerking upright with a gasp. "What—where—why am I wet?!"

"We crashed in the ocean," Sana explained calmly. "I'm currently operating as our flotation device. You're welcome."

Kronii looked around, processing this, then looked down at herself—soaked through, her usually perfect hair plastered to her face. "I look terrible."

"We crashed in the ocean," Sana repeated. "Maybe priorities?"

"My appearance is always a priority," Kronii muttered, but she was already moving, checking on the others. "Fauna? IRyS? Mumei? Bae?"

Fauna stirred first, rolling over and immediately groaning. "Water. Why is there so much water? Where are the plants? I need plants."

"Ocean," Sana supplied. "No plants here."

"I hate this already," Fauna announced.

IRyS sat up next, looking around with wide eyes. "Ocean. We're in the ocean. How—what—" She stopped, noticing Sana. "You're huge."

"Emergency survival size," Sana confirmed. "I woke up early and figured we'd all drown if I didn't. So, you know, you're welcome for not drowning."

Mumei was awake now too, immediately pulling out her phone and tapping at it despite the water damage making the screen glitch and flicker. "Interesting," she murmured.

"What's interesting?" Kronii demanded. "The fact that we're stranded in the middle of the ocean?"

"We're not in the middle of the ocean," Mumei said, still focused on her phone. "We're in the Bermuda Triangle."

Everyone froze.

"The what now?" Fauna asked slowly.

"The Bermuda Triangle," Mumei repeated, as if this was completely normal information. "You know, the area where ships and planes mysteriously disappear? Where weird supernatural stuff happens? That Bermuda Triangle."

"Oh perfect," IRyS said faintly. "That's just perfect. Because our day wasn't bad enough already."

Bae finally woke up, and her first action was to immediately reach for her dice bag, which was somehow still attached to her belt despite everything. She pulled out a die and rolled it across Sana's arm-turned-surface.

Nothing happened.

She frowned, rolled again. Still nothing.

"Uh," Bae said. "That's not good."

"What's not good?" Kronii was already moving toward her. "What's wrong?"

"My probability manipulation," Bae held up the dice. "It's not working. I mean, I can still roll the dice, and I can still move around, but the actual manipulation part? The chaos powers? Super weak. Like, barely functioning."

"My time powers feel off too," Kronii admitted, holding up her hand and attempting to create a time bubble. A small one flickered into existence, barely the size of a basketball, before dissipating. "I can do small stuff, minor speed boosts, tiny alterations, but nothing major."

"I can't use my powers at all," Fauna said, looking at her hands in dismay. "Everything I do requires nature, plants, earth. There's nothing but water here!"

IRyS tried to summon her usual divine-demonic energy, managing only to create small bullet-sized beams of light that fizzled out almost immediately. "Mine are working but extremely weakened. These are barely stronger than a paintball."

"That's what I was afraid of," Sana sighed. "I noticed it earlier. I can float and maintain this size, but I can't go any bigger. And I can barely fly—maybe four inches above the water before I lose control. The Bermuda Triangle is dampening our powers."

"Why didn't you mention this earlier?!" Kronii demanded.

"You were unconscious!" Sana shot back. "Who was I going to tell? The ocean?"

"The ocean might have been more helpful than this conversation," Fauna muttered.

Mumei was still studying her glitching phone. "According to this—and I'm reading between the distortion lines here—the Bermuda Triangle has historically interfered with navigation equipment, compasses, and according to some theories, might have some kind of electromagnetic or supernatural properties that—"

"Mumei," IRyS interrupted gently. "Maybe the summary version?"

"The Triangle makes our powers wonky and we should be concerned," Mumei condensed.

"Thank you."

Bae stood up, balancing carefully on Sana, and looked around at the endless water. "Okay, so we're stranded in the Bermuda Triangle with limited powers, no land in sight, and—"

Thunder rumbled in the distance.

They all turned slowly to look north.

A storm was forming on the horizon, dark clouds gathering with unnatural speed, and even from this distance they could see the lightning crackling through them.

"You have got to be kidding me," Kronii said flatly.

"It gets worse," Mumei said, squinting at something in the water near the storm. "What are those red things?"

Everyone looked. In the water, moving with the storm, were shapes. Large shapes. Very large shapes.

Red shapes.

"Please tell me those are boats," Fauna said weakly.

"Those are not boats," Mumei confirmed, pulling out a book from her somehow-still-dry bag. Where had she been keeping that? How was it dry? These were questions for later. "Let me check... ah. Here."

She flipped through pages rapidly, found what she was looking for, and her face went pale.

"Those are Red Orcas," she read aloud. "Also called Red Demons of the Sea. They're a subspecies of orcas that are approximately four times larger than regular orcas, can fling themselves up to forty meters out of the water, are constantly hungry, extremely aggressive, and prefer hunting in stormy weather within the Bermuda Triangle."

Silence.

"Run that by me again," Kronii said. "Four times larger?"

"Than regular orcas, which are already massive," Mumei confirmed. "These things are basically ocean kaiju."

"Forty meters out of the water?" IRyS asked faintly.

"Like dolphins, but supersized and angry."

"Constantly hungry?"

"Very."

"And there are how many of them?" Bae asked, trying to count the red shapes.

Mumei counted carefully. "Fifty. It's a full pod. All heading directly toward us with the storm."

Another moment of silence as this information processed.

"Well," Sana said with forced cheer. "This is fine. This is totally fine. We'll just—"

"SWIM!" Fauna screamed.

"I'M NOT BUILT FOR SPEED!" Sana yelled back, but she was already turning, trying to propel herself in the opposite direction from the approaching nightmare.

"Kronii, can you speed us up?" IRyS asked urgently.

"I can try!" Kronii pressed her hands against Sana, channeling what little time manipulation she could muster. Sana's movements accelerated slightly, but not by much. "This is the best I can do! It's like trying to fast-forward through mud!"

The storm was gaining on them. The red orcas were gaining faster.

"Mumei, IRyS, you two have ranged attacks!" Bae shouted. "Can you hold them off?"

"With what, my strongly worded letter beams?" IRyS shot back, but she was already turning, hands glowing with weak divine energy. "I'll try!"

Mumei had produced a tome from somewhere—again, where was she keeping these things?—and her eyes were glowing with eldritch knowledge. "Tax beams," she muttered. "I can use tax beams. They wither whatever they hit, but they're weakened like everything else."

"Tax beams?" Fauna repeated. "You're going to file their taxes aggressively?"

"The IRS is the most feared organization for a reason!" Mumei retorted. "Don't question my methods!"

The red orcas were close enough now to see details, and those details were terrifying. Each one was the size of a bus, their distinctive red coloring making them look like they'd been dipped in blood, their eyes intelligent and hungry and fixed directly on the group.

"INCOMING!" Bae yelled.

The first orca breached, launching itself out of the water with impossible height, easily clearing thirty meters before starting its descent directly toward them.

"SHOOT IT!" everyone screamed at once.

Mumei's hands glowed with sickly green energy, and she fired a beam of pure bureaucratic nightmare—the concentrated essence of tax season—directly at the orca. It hit the creature mid-arc, and visible decay spread across its skin. The orca roared in pain and confusion, splashing back into the water short of its target.

"It works!" Mumei shouted triumphantly. "Take that, aquatic tax evader!"

"Your battle cries need work!" Kronii yelled.

Two more orcas breached simultaneously. IRyS fired her weakened divine beams, the small bullets of light peppering one of them like a machine gun. It wasn't enough to seriously hurt it, but it was enough to disorient it, sending it crashing sideways.

The second orca got through, landing with a massive splash just meters from Sana, sending a wave of water over everyone.

"TOO CLOSE!" Fauna shrieked. "THAT WAS TOO CLOSE!"

"I'm going as fast as I can!" Sana yelled back, her cosmic powers straining to keep her moving and keep her enlarged form stable.

More orcas were breaching now, a coordinated assault. The pod had clearly decided these strange floating creatures were either food or a threat, and either way needed to be dealt with.

Mumei and IRyS fell into a rhythm, calling out targets and firing in tandem.

"Left, high!"

"Got it!"

"Three coming in low!"

"On it!"

"Behind us—BEHIND US!"

An orca had circled around, coming up from behind with its massive jaws open. Bae reacted on instinct, throwing one of her dice with chaos-enhanced force. The die expanded mid-flight, growing to the size of a basketball and striking the orca directly in its snout. It bellowed and dove back under.

"Did you just throw a giant die at a demon orca?" Kronii asked incredulously.

"It worked, didn't it?!" Bae shot back, already pulling out more dice.

"Fair point!"

The storm had reached them now, rain lashing down in sheets, lightning cracking overhead. The waves were getting larger, threatening to wash everyone off Sana.

"Hold on!" Sana warned. "This is about to get choppy!"

"Choppy?!" Fauna grabbed onto Sana's collar for dear life. "This is already choppy! This is beyond choppy! This is—"

A massive wave hit them, and Fauna's words dissolved into a scream as water crashed over everyone.

They came up gasping, still miraculously all on Sana, still somehow moving, still being chased by forty-plus angry red orcas through a supernatural storm in the Bermuda Triangle.

"I just want to state for the record," Kronii gasped out between waves, "that this is the worst team building exercise ever!"

"Noted!" Sana yelled back.

"Why can't I do anything?!" Fauna wailed, looking at her hands in frustration. "I'm Mother Nature! I should be able to do something!"

"You're moral support!" IRyS called back, firing another volley of weakened beams at an approaching orca. "Very important!"

"I don't want to be moral support! I want to be useful support!"

"Then be supportive!" Bae suggested. "Cheer us on!"

"You can do it, Mumei and IRyS!" Fauna cheered desperately. "Keep shooting the giant demon orcas! You're doing great!"

"That's the spirit!" Bae encouraged.

Another wave crashed over them. More orcas breached. Mumei's tax beams and IRyS's divine bullets continued their desperate suppressing fire.

Kronii kept Sana's time accelerated as much as she could manage. Bae threw chaos-enhanced dice at anything that got too close. Fauna cheered and held on for dear life.

And Sana, the cosmic entity currently serving as everyone's life raft, swam for their lives through a supernatural storm while being hunted by a pod of demon orcas.

"Next vacation," Sana gasped out, "we're going somewhere with solid ground!"

"Agreed!" everyone chorused.

The chase continued, the storm raged on, and somewhere in the back of all their minds was the same thought: The others better be having an easier time than this.
