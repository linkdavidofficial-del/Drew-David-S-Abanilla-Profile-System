Chapter 9: The Long Road Home — Part Two: Triangle Triumph (Council + IRyS's Power Surge)

The Long Road Home
Part Two: Triangle Triumph (Council + IRyS's Power Surge)

The red orcas were gaining, the storm was raging, and Sana was running out of cosmic energy to maintain both her size and speed.

"I'M GOING AS FAST AS I CAN!" she yelled, her massive form cutting through the water as she essentially became a living speedboat for her friends.

"FASTER!" Fauna shrieked, holding onto Sana's collar for dear life.

Mumei and IRyS continued their desperate suppressing fire, tax beams and divine bullets peppering the approaching pod of demon orcas. Each hit slowed them slightly, caused them to veer off course, but there were so many and the girls' powers were still weakened by the Triangle.

"We can't keep this up forever!" IRyS called out between volleys.

"We don't need forever!" Kronii shouted back, maintaining her time acceleration on Sana. "We just need to get out of the Triangle's influence!"

Bae was throwing dice like her life depended on it—which it did—each one expanding mid-flight to strike approaching orcas with chaos-enhanced impacts. "How far are we from the edge?!"

Mumei, still clutching her glitching phone, squinted at the screen. "According to this—and I'm reading through about seventeen different error messages—we're close! Maybe a mile!"

"A MILE?!" Fauna wailed. "That's an eternity!"

"It's sixty-three seconds at our current speed!" Mumei corrected.

"THAT'S STILL TOO LONG!"

Another red orca breached, closer than before, its massive jaws snapping shut mere feet from Sana's head.

"TOO CLOSE!" Sana screamed, swerving. "THAT WAS WAY TOO CLOSE!"

"Keep moving!" IRyS urged, firing another volley. But her beams were flickering, growing weaker. "I'm running out of power!"

"Me too," Mumei gasped, her tax beams diminishing to barely more than sparklers.

"We're not going to make it," Bae said quietly, still throwing dice but knowing it wasn't enough.

"YES WE ARE!" Kronii's eyes blazed. "Nobody dies today! Not on my watch!"

She pushed harder, straining her time manipulation to its absolute limit, forcing Sana's movements to accelerate beyond what should be possible.

The strain was visible on Kronii's face, sweat mixing with sea spray, but she didn't let up.

Forty-five seconds.

The orcas were so close now they could see individual teeth, could smell the creature's breath.

Thirty seconds.

Mumei's phone began to clear, the glitching subsiding slightly.

"Wait," she said, looking at the device. "Wait, the interference is decreasing!"

Twenty seconds.

IRyS tried another shot and gasped. "My beam is stronger! It's working properly!"

Fifteen seconds.

"I CAN FLY!" Sana suddenly shouted, her voice filled with hope as her levitation powers kicked back in properly. She wasn't just swimming anymore—she was gliding, her massive form rising slightly out of the water.

Ten seconds.

"ALMOST THERE!" Kronii yelled.

Five seconds.

The orcas lunged, all of them, a coordinated final assault—

And then they crossed the border.

The moment they left the Bermuda Triangle's influence, everything changed.

Their powers surged back to full strength like a dam breaking. Mumei's phone cleared completely. IRyS's beams blazed with renewed divine fury. Kronii's time manipulation snapped into perfect clarity. Bae's chaos energy exploded around her. Fauna felt her connection to nature slam back into place, suddenly able to sense every piece of algae and seaweed in the entire ocean around them.

And Sana grew.

Massive. Cosmic. Her true size unleashed, expanding until she towered above the waves, fifty times larger than before, a genuine stellar entity no longer constrained.

They all stopped, turning as one to face their pursuers.

The red orcas, emerging from the Triangle's border in pursuit, suddenly found themselves facing something very different from fleeing prey.

"Okay," Kronii said, cracking her neck with deliberate menace. "NOW it's our turn."

Bae pulled out a handful of D20s, all of them glowing with chaotic energy, a manic grin on her face. "Who's the prey now?"

Sana, now large enough that the orcas looked like normal-sized fish next to her, loomed over the pod. Her voice, when she spoke, echoed across the water. "Time for some payback."

Mumei's eyes blazed with eldritch knowledge, her connection to civilization's darkest aspects fully restored. "They messed with the wrong idols."

IRyS rose into the air, wings of light and darkness manifesting, her full Hope and Despair powers radiating. "Let's teach them a lesson."

And Fauna, despite still being surrounded by water, smiled. A dangerous, deadly smile. "I may be at a disadvantage," she said sweetly, "but my range is unlimited now. And there's seaweed everywhere."

The red orcas, perhaps sensing that the tables had dramatically turned, hesitated.

Too late.

"CHARGE!" Kronii bellowed, and they charged.

What followed was less a fight and more a complete reversal of the previous chase.

Mumei went first, her war cry echoing across the waves as she unleashed a barrage of full-strength tax beams. The green energy lanced out, striking orca after orca, each hit causing visible withering and decay. "THIS IS FOR CHASING US!" she screamed, firing rapidly. "TAX EVASION IS NOT TOLERATED!"

IRyS joined her, singing a battle hymn as she alternated between hope and despair beams. The divine energy—no longer weakened to bullets but restored to full laser intensity—carved through the water, each shot powerful enough to send orcas tumbling.

Kronii was a blur of motion, creating massive time bubbles that froze individual orcas in place. Then, with a roar of "MUDA MUDA MUDA MUDA!" she proceeded to punch them repeatedly while they were helpless, each strike phased to hit despite the time stop. It was excessive. It was dramatic. It was extremely cathartic.

Sana, at her full cosmic size, was performing what could only be described as WWE moves on creatures that were now relatively small compared to her. She grabbed an orca in each hand, lifted them high, and performed a perfect suplex, slamming both of them back into the water simultaneously. "THAT'S FOR TRYING TO EAT US!"

Another orca tried to flee. Sana caught it mid-swim and executed a picture-perfect powerbomb. "WHERE DO YOU THINK YOU'RE GOING?!"

"Is she... is she doing wrestling moves on demon orcas?" Fauna asked, momentarily distracted from her own assault.

"Don't question it," Bae advised, rolling her D20s. Each die exploded with chaotic energy, creating random effects—one orca suddenly turned neon pink, another began moving in reverse, a third found itself inexplicably wearing a tiny hat. "Chaos does what chaos wants!"

Fauna, despite the water disadvantage, was making it work. She reached out with her nature powers, finding every strand of seaweed, every piece of algae within miles. With a shriek of vindictive glee, she commanded them to rise.

From the depths, massive tangles of seaweed erupted, wrapping around the red orcas like tentacles. The creatures thrashed and fought, but Fauna's rage was infinite and her control absolute. "CHAIN THEM!" she commanded, and the seaweed obeyed, binding the orcas tight.

"FAUNA'S SCARY WHEN SHE'S MAD!" Bae called out, rolling more dice.

"THAT'S WHAT THEY GET FOR MAKING ME USELESS!" Fauna yelled back, her usual gentle demeanor completely replaced with Mother Nature's Wrath.

The battle—if it could even be called that—was completely one-sided. The red orcas, apex predators of the Bermuda Triangle, found themselves thoroughly outmatched by five very angry idols with supernatural powers and scores to settle.

Within minutes, the entire pod was either fleeing back toward the Triangle or wrapped up in seaweed and floating helplessly.

"Anyone else?!" Kronii called out to the fleeing orcas. "No?! That's what I thought!"

They regrouped, breathing hard but victorious. The storm from the Triangle still raged in the distance, but out here, beyond its influence, the water was calm and their powers were strong.

"We did it," IRyS said, slight disbelief in her voice. "We actually survived the Bermuda Triangle."

"And beat up demon orcas," Mumei added.

"Don't forget the seaweed chains," Fauna said proudly, admiring her work.

"I think I threw a hundred dice," Bae calculated. "So many dice."

"I performed at least seven different wrestling moves," Sana reported, shrinking back down to a more reasonable size. "That was weirdly satisfying."

Kronii pulled out her phone—now functioning perfectly—and pulled up a map. "Okay, we're... actually not that far from home. If we fly, we can be back at the EN building in maybe four hours."

"Let's never take a vacation by plane again," Fauna said immediately.

"Seconded," everyone chorused.

"For at least several months," IRyS added.

"Several months is not nearly long enough," Mumei pointed out. "I'm thinking years."

"Forever," Bae suggested. "Let's just say forever."

"Forever works," Kronii agreed.

They took a moment to celebrate properly—cheering, high-fiving, even a group hug that nearly sent them all into the water because Sana hadn't quite stabilized her size yet.

Then they turned toward home and flew.

Six idols, soaking wet, exhausted, but victorious, heading back to civilization with a story that literally nobody would believe.

"Think anyone will believe us about the red orcas?" IRyS asked as they flew.

"No," came the immediate unanimous response.

"Fair enough."
