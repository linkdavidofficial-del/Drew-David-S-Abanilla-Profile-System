Epilogue: Scattered Across the World

Somewhere in a vast desert, five members of Myth ran from a sandstorm, screaming and carrying a dehydrated shark, while one of them had wisely decided to run in the opposite direction.

Somewhere in the Bermuda Triangle, six girls floated on an enlarged cosmic entity, fighting off demon orcas with weakened powers and sheer determination.

Somewhere on a tropical island, five idols built shelter and explored their surprisingly abundant new home, already making survival plans.

And somewhere in a mountain resort, four exhausted warriors finally found warmth and safety after battling cryptids in the snow.

They were scattered across the globe, separated by thousands of miles and vastly different survival situations.

But they were alive.

They were together with their groups.

And somehow, someday, they would reunite.

Until then, they would survive, adapt, and probably continue to have the worst luck possible while doing so.

Because they were Hololive EN.

And nothing—not plane crashes, not suicidal pigeons, not detaching aircraft, not deserts or oceans or islands or mountains—could keep them down for long.

The adventure was just beginning.
